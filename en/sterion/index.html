02 / Safety and test evidence

Four functions; evidence before claims.

01 / REMOVE

Physical particle removal

Air transport and extraction testing. Surface removal is measured separately from actual filtration capture.

02 / ACTIVE

Surface treatment research

Assessment of antimicrobial processes targeting inner footwear surfaces. No demonstrated efficacy yet.

03 / VERIFY

Control, containment and testing

Interlocks, traceable measurements and independent validation before making any disinfection claim.

No sterilization, virus or fungal elimination efficacy, or safety certification has been demonstrated. UV-C for CORE requires validated containment and safety interlocks; INSERT must not emit UV-C into an open shoe.

04 / Next engineering milestones

From drawings to tested hardware.

  1. G1
    Select actual parts and confirm interfaces
    Supplier datasheets, holes, gaskets, fasteners, and dimensional assembly tests.
  2. G2
    Measure pressure, flow, and particle capture
    Real hardware data for airflow, electrical power, sound, and inert particle balance.
  3. G3
    Design a contained antimicrobial process
    Treatment selection, containment, material compatibility, and appropriate safety engineering.
  4. G4
    Independent microbiological validation
    Material-specific and location-specific reduction tests in qualified laboratories.
  5. G5
    Release an actual manufacturing revision
    Only after controlled drawings, verified components, and material risks are resolved.
