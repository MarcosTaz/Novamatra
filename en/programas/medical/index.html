MOBILITY / ASSISTIVE RESEARCH

Medical-assistive exoskeleton

Investigate mechanical mobility and rehabilitation assistance principles, clearly separating research from a medical device.

Exploratory research
Core question

What assistance can be safely controlled, measured and adapted to individual needs?

Ambition

Explore assistive systems with possible future clinical relevance, subject to independent evaluation and applicable regulation.

What is needed

Clinical researchers, physiotherapists, biomechanists, safety engineers, ethical protocols and regulatory validation.

Next required evidence

First a non-clinical mechanical demonstrator, risk assessment and expert review before any human trials.

Non-negotiable constraints

Not an approved medical device; no therapeutic claims or unauthorized clinical testing.
