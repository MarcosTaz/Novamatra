NOVAMATRA / RESEARCH PROGRAMME / ENERGY

RF300.

Investigating energy conversion in controlled fluid flows.

Standalone energy engineering programme. The current phase includes a virtual study environment and preliminary air-flow calculations. No steam-performance demonstration, pressure-rated prototype or validated net output exists.

COMPUTATIONAL STUDY / CFD NOT VALIDATED

Geometry and instrumentation

Comparison of architectures and definition of pressure, flow and speed measurements.

Evidence criteria

Model assumptions are not experimental results; fluid properties, boundary conditions, loss models and safety testing remain necessary.

Engineering drawings, archives and algorithms remain confidential and outside the public repository.
