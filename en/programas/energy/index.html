ENERGY / CONVERSION

Energy systems

Research modular energy conversion from human motion, wind, water and external sources — without claiming energy multiplication.

Exploratory research
Core question

How can more useful energy be recovered from real, irregular inputs while accounting for every loss?

Ambition

Develop robust, repairable systems adaptable to different inputs, with demonstrated performance and practical applications.

What is needed

Characterizable generators, transmissions, shafts, bearings, torque sensors, power electronics and a test bench.

Next required evidence

Build an actual bench, measure synchronized mechanical work and electrical energy, compare with a baseline.

Non-negotiable constraints

Conservation of energy, overspeed, starting loads, losses, variable wind/water and electrical safety.

OPEN INDUSTRY RESEARCH CHALLENGE · Energy · motors · transmissions

A real energy source. An unambiguous measurement.

We aim to evaluate human and environmental motion conversion without hiding mechanical work or losses.

01 / First verifiable outcome

Measure mechanical input energy, useful electricity and losses on a low-voltage bench against a reference.

02 / Skills and materials needed

Loose motors, shafts, bearings, power electronics, torque sensors and metrology expertise.
