NOVAMATRA · ASSISTIVE SYSTEMS

COPILOT 360

Research into mobility assistance for blind and visually impaired people, combining spatial perception, pedestrian route assessment and offline audio guidance.

Software research and prototyping — not validated for real-world assistive use.

Research areas

01

Spatial perception and stereo geometry

02

Pedestrian route planning and hazard anticipation

03

Deterministic supervisor and uncertainty management

04

Audio guidance and offline commands

Evidence and current status

Software components and reproducible synthetic tests exist. Real-world perception, wearable hardware performance and physical assistive safety have not yet been sufficiently validated. This system must not replace a white cane, guide dog or human assistance.

Research method

Development follows modular isolation, regression testing, independent comparison of public technologies and preservation of original code. Experimental outcomes do not constitute certification.
