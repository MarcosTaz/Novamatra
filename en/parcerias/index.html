RESEARCH CHALLENGES · INDUSTRIAL CONVERGENCE

The next breakthrough may not belong to one industry.

It may emerge when technologies, materials and teams that rarely meet work on the same question. NOVAMATRA seeks to open that conversation — with respect, ambition and measurable standards.

THREE WAYS TO CONTRIBUTE

A first conversation can be modest. The question should matter.

01 / RESEARCH

Propose a technical challenge

A specific limitation in energy, materials, movement, control or safety that might benefit from another discipline's perspective.

First step: define the problem and one decisive measurement.
02 / MATERIALS

Explore reusable industrial resources

Raw materials, identified production offcuts or loose components, lawfully available before they enter regulated waste streams.

First step: identify type, condition, origin and availability.
03 / CO-DEVELOPMENT

Evaluate joint experimental research

Bring together manufacturers, laboratories, universities and specialist knowledge to assess a shared hypothesis against clear criteria.

First step: agree scope, responsibilities and conditions.

HOW WE BEGIN

A technical conversation, without advance commitments.

We begin with a non-confidential description of the problem, materials or expertise required, and a proposed test. Reserved technical details require appropriate safeguards.

01 / SCOPE

A specific need, skill or identifiable resource.

02 / ASSESSMENT

Objectives, safety, legal conditions, resources and measurement methods.

03 / DECISION

Proceed, revise or reject the hypothesis transparently. No exclusivity or IP transfer is assumed.
